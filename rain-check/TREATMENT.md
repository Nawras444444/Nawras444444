# RAIN CHECK: 30 s, 80s Cel Anime

**Brief (user):** anime look, fonts and colours from the supplied palette (Xanthous #F7B538 + Burgundy #780116, bold condensed display type), a 30 s story: a man walks a road far from the city, forgets his umbrella, the weather turns to storm, he has to find a way to stay dry.
**Defaults I chose:** English; no voiced dialogue (a deadpan silent hero) with three short burned-in "thought" subtitles; 1920x1080, 24 fps, full 16:9; no storyboard stop; score and foley synthesised, no samples.
**Style:** `cel-anime-80s` (STYLE.md kept intact). The uploaded caricature photo is used for *mood only* (flat saturated colour field, deadpan thinking beat). It looks like a real person, so the hero is an original design and no likeness is drawn.

## 1. Three candidate structures
A. **Straight journey**: walk, forget, storm, failed fixes, success. Clear, but it is the cliché, and the ending is just "he is dry".
B. **Rewind tape**: open inside the storm, the VCR rewinds to the sunny walk, then plays forward. Clever, but it eats the 3-second hook and spends our only tape artefact at the start.
C. **One road, three colour models** (STYLE native move: *colour-model change*): the *same* wide composition of the road is painted three times: golden Xanthous dusk, Burgundy storm, washed afterglow. Between the first and last frame the hero's problem is solved and the world has changed; the final shot is the opening shot **repainted**.

**Chosen: C**, built on a short A spine. It needs no dialogue, the palette from the references becomes the *story* (Xanthous = calm, Burgundy = threat), and the ending echoes the opening by repainting it.

## 2. Logline and arc
A small man in a big golden evening forgets the one thing that mattered, and the sky turns burgundy on him; he runs out of clever ideas and finally takes the answer the road had been offering all along.
- Setup (0-4 s): Xanthous dusk, steady walk, city glowing far behind.
- Turn (4-8 s): empty arm, the sky shifts to burgundy, one silent beat, the first raindrop.
- Struggle (8-14 s): briefcase, newspaper and jacket all fail, escalating to a lightning impact frame.
- Resolve (14-24 s): the giant leaf; eyes, tear, raise; held peak under the downpour; title.
- Ending (26-30 s): the opening road repainted in afterglow, he walks off under his leaf, the tape runs out.

## 3. Benchmark
- **Learn from:** the rain-shelter sequences of 80s/90s Japanese family animation (tiny figure under a huge object; rain heard before it is seen; a silence before the first drop), and city-pop OVA openings (backlit titles, held painted stills, hard-edged cel shading).
- **Not taken:** any character, design, shot, title or melody from those works. Hero, prop gags, title and tune are original.

## 4. Look decisions
- Hero **accent colour = Xanthous** (pocket square, briefcase strap, rim light) so he pops on both the golden and burgundy plates. Suit deep ink-navy with violet line, burgundy tie. Skin gets its own colour in each lighting set.
- Plates: A golden dusk (Xanthous to peach to violet at the horizon, far city glow); B storm (Burgundy to near-black plum, wet road); C afterglow (pale Xanthous, rainbow band, puddle reflections).
- Leaf: muted jade, flat 2-tone, backlit by lightning.
- **Type:** title in a heavy condensed display face (Anton, OFL), chrome gradient re-keyed to the user's palette (Xanthous, white horizon line, Burgundy), neon outline, katakana/kanji line in Dela Gothic One (OFL) reading 雨宿り (*amayadori*, "taking shelter from the rain"). Subtitles in Barlow Semi Condensed 600, cream-yellow with dark outline, ~96 px from bottom. Pill-shaped outline tags (like the reference swatch cards) appear as the small "page / bar" HUD marks in the corner at the title.
- CRT/VHS pass per STYLE.md; one tracking-noise accent (26.0 s, the repaint) and one tape run-out (29.4 s).
